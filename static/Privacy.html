Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Movie Finder ("we," "us," "our," or "Service") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Username/Name: You provide this when creating an account or logging in
  • Access Code: Used for authentication and account security

1.2 Search and Activity Data

  • Search Queries: The movie titles or descriptions you search for
  • Movie Ratings: Your star ratings (1-5) for movies you rate
  • Search Mode: Whether you search by title or by description/vibe
  • Interaction Logs: Timestamps and metadata about your searches and ratings

1.3 Technical Information

  • Browser type and version
  • IP address (for security and service improvement)
  • Device type (mobile, tablet, desktop)
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Authentication: To verify your identity and grant you access to the Service
  • Movie Recommendations: To provide personalized movie recommendations based on your search queries and ratings
  • Service Improvement: To analyze user behavior and improve our search algorithm and user experience
  • Data Analysis: To understand what types of movies users are interested in
  • Security: To detect and prevent fraudulent activity or abuse
  • Communication: To send you updates, technical notices, or support responses (if applicable)

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. However, we may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • Third-Party APIs: We use external movie data services (e.g., TMDB) to provide recommendations. Movie data is anonymized before being sent to these services
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government authority
  • Service Providers: We may share information with vendors who help us operate the Service (hosting, analytics). These vendors are bound by confidentiality agreements
  • Business Transfer: If we merge, are acquired, or sell assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction

4. Data Retention

We retain your account information and search/rating data as long as your account remains active. If you close your account or request deletion, we will remove your personal data within 30 days, except where required by law or for legitimate business purposes (e.g., fraud prevention).

Anonymized Data: We may retain anonymized, aggregated data (e.g., "5,000 users rated this movie highly") for analysis and service improvement.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: You can request to update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt-Out: You can disable data collection features (e.g., disable ratings) if applicable
  • Portability: You can request your data in a portable, machine-readable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at [your-email@example.com].

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Secure password hashing and storage
  • Access controls and authentication protocols

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. Use a strong, unique password and never share your access code with others.

7. Third-Party Links

Our Service may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., IMDb, movie databases). This Privacy Policy does not apply to those websites. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before sharing any information with them.

8. Children's Privacy

Movie Finder is not intended for users under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete such information and terminate the child's account immediately.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date above. Your continued use of the Service after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
